About This Game

Objective: collect notebooks while avoiding Baldi.

Use WASD or arrow keys to move, and hold Shift to sprint while you have stamina.

Pick up coins and spend them at vending machines for temporary boosts.

Survive, explore the maze, and gather as many notebooks as you can before Baldi catches you.
